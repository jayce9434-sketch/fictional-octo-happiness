CHORUS ISLES — COMPLETE GAME 1.0

An original musical creature-collecting game inspired by the island orchestra,
breeding, and progression loop of My Singing Monsters. All creature art, names,
compositions, and instrument samples in this package are original. This is an
independent fan-style project, not an official My Singing Monsters release.

QUICK GITHUB PAGES SETUP
1. Extract this ZIP. All files are at the root; there are no internal folders.
2. Upload ALL extracted files directly into your GitHub repository root.
3. Open repository Settings > Pages.
4. Set Source to Deploy from a branch, select main and / (root), then Save.
5. Open the published GitHub Pages address after the deployment finishes.
6. On iPad, open it in Safari. Share > Add to Home Screen gives it an app icon.
7. Tap Start my orchestra, or tap the sound button, to enable audio.

No installation, paid packages, CDN, database, API keys, or account is needed.
You can also open index.html locally on a computer. Hosted HTTPS is required
for service-worker offline support. A Python local preview is optional:
  python3 -m http.server 8080

START PLAYING
- You begin with Pluff and Bouldrum, 25,000 coins, 120 gems, 5,000 food, 20 stars,
  and a free Dewdrop egg. Open Breed > Nursery and hatch that egg.
- Tap Pluff and Bouldrum on your island. Feed each three times to reach level 4.
- Breed them for Mossknock. The first breeding takes only 20 seconds.
- Collect coins. Complete and claim the early Journey quests.
- Buy other starter eggs and follow the exact recipes in the Voice Journal.
- Use the Bakery to grow the orchestra. Upgrade beds before hatching big voices.

THE BILLION-COIN ROUTE
Perform > World Tour: hit 2,000 notes within 20 active minutes to earn:
  10,000,000,000 coins
  25,000 gems
  100,000 stars
  50,000,000 food
The coin and food rewards double for each additional island you own.
The challenge saves successful notes and remaining time. Pause it to take a
break. Reloading or closing the game leaves the challenge safely paused.
The same challenge can be repeated. There is no real-money shop or advertising.

THE INFINITY VAULT
Own all seven islands and win three World Tours. Claim this Journey quest or
open the vault from the Perform menu to receive:
  1,000,000,000,000 coins (ONE TRILLION)
  1,000,000,000 gems (ONE BILLION)
  1,000,000,000 stars (ONE BILLION)
  1,000,000,000 food (ONE BILLION)
This is a permanent, one-time endgame reward on each save.

CONTROLS
Touch: tap a creature or building. Drag the island to pan. Pinch to zoom.
Move: select a creature > Move, then tap open ground. Use Cancel to keep its spot.
Desktop: mouse drag to pan, wheel to zoom. The + / - buttons also work.
Performance: tap the three lane pads when notes reach the glowing hit line.
Keyboard performance keys: A / S / D or Left / Down / Right. Held keys do not
auto-play. Close or use Pause & save to take a break without losing the run.

INCLUDED
- 7 illustrated floating islands, each with a different musical scale and tempo.
- 38 original species, including 4 Ethereals, 2 Titans, and 6 seasonal species.
- 8 different melodic/rhythmic phrases per species. These are actual different
  note sequences, not copies of a one-note loop.
- Locally synthesized choir vowels, felt piano, bells, flute, bass, strings,
  brass, reed organ, plucked strings, kick, snare, and shakers. Soft delay and
  dynamic compression keep large orchestras balanced.
- Per-creature mute, phrase selection, costumes, and 0–2000% voice volume.
- Separate 0–2000% master, music, and sound-effects settings.
- Common, Rare (2x income), and Epic (5x income) variants.
- Recipe journal, permanent wishing torches, and bad-luck protection.
- 31 Journey quests, 5 daily quests, daily gifts, and daily gift streaks.
- 3 repeatable rhythm performances, 3 expedition routes, and 2 Titan awakenings.
- 12 placeable decorations, 6 shared costumes, and 3 earned-currency crates.
- Breeding nest, hall capacity, oven, gem mine, and wishing torch upgrades.
- Up to 350 creatures globally and up to 60 decorations per island.
- Creatures store up to 12 hours of coins, including offline earnings.
- Breeding, baking, mines, nursery, and expeditions finish while closed.
- No individual breeding, nursery, bakery, or expedition wait exceeds 1 hour.
- Touch support, responsive portrait/landscape UI, app icons, battery-saver mode,
  automatic saving, checksum-protected export/import, and offline caching.

EVENTS
The Hollow Chorus: October 1–31, ends November 1, following the device calendar.
  Pumpkinpip: Pluff + Bouldrum.
  Spectrabel: Dewdrop + Petalune.
  Hexmoth: Cinderling + Skyrill.
The Golden Leaf Festival: September 1–November 30, ends December 1.
  Maplemaw: Pluff + Skyrill.
  Acornote: Bouldrum + Mossknock.
  Ochrefox: Petalune + Reedling.
Earn event tokens through play or use exact seasonal recipes. Token exchanges
offer guaranteed Common, Rare, and Epic event eggs. Earned creatures, costumes,
decorations, and remaining tokens stay saved after the event ends. Events return
annually. Seasonal species can only be first acquired during an active season.

ROOMS
Public mode uses nine locally simulated musician bots. They answer questions
about recipes, progress, money, and events; these are not real online players.
A room code with five or more letters/numbers creates a private room with zero
bots. Rooms share your current saved orchestra. No server setup is required.

SAVE YOUR ORCHESTRA
The game saves to this browser/device automatically. Export from Settings to
keep a backup or transfer to another device. Clearing browser/site data removes
the local save. Safari and a Home Screen app can have separate storage; use
Export/Import to move progress between them if needed.
Save imports are checked and previewed before replacing the current orchestra.
Offline earnings are capped at 12 hours per return. Creature banks also cap at
12 hours of their production. Export your save before replacing project files.

Every file in this package belongs directly in the repository root.
